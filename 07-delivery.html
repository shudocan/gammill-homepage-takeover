Gammill Delivery Technicians Deliver, Set up, & Train in your home

You bought your machine to quilt - not to spend weeks fighting with it before it works. Some longarm manufacturers drop a crate on your porch and the rest is up to you.

Not Gammill! 100% of our machines are delivered & set up in your home by full-time Gammill Delivery Techs

Delivered, set up, and quilting

A substantial visit by a Gammill employee who is an expert in getting you Quilting with Confidence!

Installed by a full-time Gammill technician.

A Gammill machine isn't shipped to your driveway in a box for you to assemble. It's installed in your home - by a full-time, salaried Gammill delivery technician at no additional charge. They arrive on schedule, carry out your old machine if you have one, and they aren't done until your Gammill is in your space, level, calibrated, your basic training is completed, and you're stitching beautifully.

Dialed in to your room.

During the visit, the technician customizes the setup to your room: table location in the room, table height, lighting optimized, and everything leveled perfectly. Even on a sloped basement floor or a second-story room with seasonal flex, we level the table to true. Then they walk you through the same materials you learned in your "Getting Started" videos, but this time hands-on, then you take your first stitches. You'll be comfortable using the basic functions and even some of the advanced ones, from the very first day.

Quilting on Day 1.

A few customers choose to do a "test quilt" for their first project, but the majority complete their first REAL quilt on Day 1.

The relationship starts here.

The technician who installs your machine isn't the last Gammill person you'll meet - they're the fourth. You already met the Product Expert who helped you order the machine, the Fulfillment Expert who handled the logistics of scheduling the delivery, the On-Boarding Expert who got you connected to your learning resources so you would be ready to run your machine from the very first day.

After the Delivery Technician leaves, you continue to be supported throughout your ownership by Gammill Education Team, Individual Educators, the Customer Service team and the Tech Support Team. Once every 5 years if you want to keep up on recommended Preventative Maintenance, you'll meet one of our Field Service Technicians.

The relationship starts here. It doesn't end here.

  • 100% delivered and set up by Gammill technicians (no shipping company or "dealer" involved)
  • Customized to your room: height, lighting, table location, leveling
  • Hands-on training before the technician leaves
  • Old machine removed if you have one
  • Everything included in the price (extra charge for Alaska, Hawaii, and international)
