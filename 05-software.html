Why Gammill - Chapter 5

We invented Computerized Quilting, then perfected it.

CreativeStudio™ is simple to learn and you'll be finishing beautiful quilts from the very first day. But because it's also powerful, you'll never outgrow it.

Statler · Statler Ascend

Every Feature, included. Free updates, forever.

CreativeStudio handles the full vocabulary of computerized quilting: edge-to-edge, blocks, repeat patterns, point-to-point, echo, fill, draw trims, border corners, resize, rotate, flip, mirror, nodes, circular array, combine, and order-join. None of it sold separately. None of it added in a paid upgrade later.

Statler and Statler Ascend both use CreativeStudio for stitching, as well as on-screen and on-quilt designing.

Updates: Gammill has never charged for CreativeStudio - or for any upgrade since the software was first released. A 2008 Statler runs the same CreativeStudio as a 2026 Ascend. Classes and seminars and friendships are so much easier when we're all using the same "version" of software!

  • CreativeStudio at the stitcher (Statler, Statler Ascend)
  • Pattern preview - see exactly where stitches will land before the first stitch (great for getting customer approval before you commit)
  • Resilient workflow - auto-restart at exact pattern position after a thread break or bobbin change; auto-calculate rows, repeats, and pattern fit without having to do math
  • CreativeStudio also includes a "Stand-Alone Mode" we call SAM. SAM works with any computerized Gammill including Elevate. Design and plan your next quilt on your laptop or desktop PC with SAM, while the current one is still being stitched.
  • 30 years of continuous improvement driven by owner feedback and requests
  • Every Gammill owner runs the same software - newbie or 20-year veteran - which is what lets the community help each other
