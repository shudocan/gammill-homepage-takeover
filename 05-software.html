Designed flat. Built to forgive. Free forever.

CreativeStudio is built around one promise: the design on the screen is the quilt that comes off your machine. What you see is what you quilt. Nothing buried. Nothing hidden in a sub-menu. Nothing to guess.

It's flat. Not buried in menus.

Most quilting software buries the controls that matter - a setting nested three menus deep, a parameter in a pop-up inside a pop-up. So when something doesn't look right, you're not quilting. You're hunting. Why isn't this working?

CreativeStudio is designed flat. You work on one screen with the tools and settings in view - and the design on that screen is the quilt that comes off your machine. Nothing buried. Nothing to guess.

Everything happens in one workspace.

Some software makes you stop mid-project, switch to a second program to draw or edit a pattern, then send your work back. Every handoff is a chance for something to go wrong.

In CreativeStudio, designing, placing, editing, and quilting all live in the same workspace. Draw an arc. Echo a pattern. Set continuous text. Fit a design to a block. You never leave the screen you started on - because there's nowhere else to go.

More than one way to reach any design.

CreativeStudio usually gives you three or four ways to reach the same result. That's not clutter - it's flexibility. Depending on the quilt in front of you, one tool is the quick path and another is the precise one - and you choose. Make any pattern elastic with nodes or with grey anchors. Stretch it, reshape it, nudge it directly, with nothing standing between you and the design.

Rigid software forces a single workflow and breaks the moment your quilt isn't the textbook case. CreativeStudio bends instead of breaking.

Small mistakes stay small.

In a lot of quilting software, one wrong move means backing out through menus - or starting the whole setup over. A small mistake becomes a big setback.

CreativeStudio is built to forgive. Undo button for fixing what you didn't mean to do. Watch a true preview before a single stitch is made. And trust that a tool does what its name says - mirror actually mirrors, trim actually trims - so your time goes into quilting, not troubleshooting.

Same software since 1995. Free updates, forever.

Statler and Statler Ascend both use CreativeStudio. A 2008 Statler runs the same CreativeStudio as a 2026 Ascend. Gammill has never charged for CreativeStudio - or for any update since the software was first released. Thirty years of continuous improvement, driven by owner feedback. Classes and seminars and friendships are so much easier when we're all using the same "version" of software.

Built for every kind of quilting.

  • Design - Draw arcs, lines, curves, and freehand · Record your free-hand motions to use again · Make patterns elastic with nodes · Sew continuous text
  • Place - Auto-fit a pattern to a boundary or block · Repeat patterns across a row or the whole quilt · Arrange patterns around a point with circular array · Apply patterns along any shape
  • Refine - Echo a pattern · Mirror a pattern · Use trim to quilt around appliqué · Stretch, rotate, flip, divide, center, nudge, and squeeze · Fill inside or outside a boundary · Convert a pattern to a boundary
  • Manage - Organize patterns with PatternCloud · Combine patterns for continuous sewing · Load an image to plan a design or create a template · Measure space with an interactive measuring function
  • Workflow - Pattern preview before the first stitch · Auto-restart at exact position after a thread break or bobbin change · Auto-calculate rows, repeats, and pattern fit without doing math · SAM (Stand-Alone Mode) on your laptop or desktop, works with any computerized Gammill including Elevate

Pattern types: point-to-point · edge-to-edge · block · border · corner · fill · text
